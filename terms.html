Key points at a glance

  • Active Trip: a trip started in the App that has not yet ended.
  • Share My Location: the App feature that lets you share your location with your Organization for a limited period.
  • Geofenced Location: an area defined by your Organization for which the App issues proximity alerts.

7. Location

7.1 Share My Location

If you choose Share My Location in the App, Safego365 tracks your location for 24 hours. The service then stops automatically.

7.2 Active Trips

During an Active Trip, location must be enabled and cannot be disabled. Your location is shared until the trip ends. You cannot use the App for an Active Trip with location turned off.

7.3 Using the App without location

You can use Safego365 with location disabled. Enabling location in the App settings does not by itself mean your location is collected or visible to Administrators. Location is collected only as described in this section and in the Privacy Policy.

7.4 Tracking requirement

For Safego365 to track your location and send location updates to your Organization’s Administrators, location must be enabled on your device. When tracking is active, your device location is collected every 2 seconds.

7.5 Geofence alerts

When you come within 1 kilometer of a Geofenced Location, you and your Organization’s Administrators are notified by push notification and SMS.

8. Safety features

8.1 SOS requests. You can send an SOS request through the App. The system records the time between your request and the acknowledgement from the responsible person or team.

8.2 Notifications. You may receive notifications about safe routes added, changes to safety recommendations, staff-related alerts, and acknowledgements of your SOS requests.

8.3 Offline contact. The App lets you call or send an SMS to your Organization’s First Responders when you have no internet connection.

9. Optional emergency profile
