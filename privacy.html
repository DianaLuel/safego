Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective date: [insert date]

Safego365 is provided by ARC ICT Technologies PLC, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

What this policy covers

Safego365 collects limited personal information to provide account, safety, trip, and emergency-support features.

Location is collected only when you use Share My Location or when you are on an Active Trip.

Your emergency profile is optional and is shared only when an emergency is triggered.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information Safego365 collects, how it is used, and who can see it. We collect only limited information about Users, and we protect it in line with global and Ethiopian data protection standards. Safego365 is designed to comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Ethiopian Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024.

While tracking is active, your device location is collected every 2 seconds. When you come within 1 kilometer of a Geofenced Location, you and your Administrators are notified by push notification and SMS.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • provide sign-in and account services, including password reset by email;
  • deliver safety notifications, alerts, and route recommendations from your Organization;
  • process SOS requests and measure the time to acknowledgement;
  • support trip monitoring and geofence alerts; and
  • share your optional emergency profile when an emergency is triggered.
5. Who can see your information

Your personal information is not visible to your Organization, to ARC ICT Technologies PLC developers, or to any third party, and we do not share it with third parties, except as described below.

5.1 Emergency information

If an emergency is triggered, your optional emergency profile is shared so you can be helped.

5.2 Location during tracking

While you are using Share My Location or are on an Active Trip, your Organization’s authorized Administrators can see your location so they can monitor and support your safety.

5.3 De-identified data

Only de-identified data is visible to your Organization’s staff and to the service provider, for example for reporting on safety activity.

6. Your emergency profile

Adding emergency contacts, blood type, allergies, and emergency notes is your choice. You can add, edit, or remove this information in the profile section of the App at any time. If you do not add it, it cannot be shared.

7. Data protection and compliance

We apply technical and organizational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. We collect only the information needed to provide the service. Safego365 is designed to comply with GDPR and with the Ethiopian Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024.

8. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed to provide the service or as the law requires.
